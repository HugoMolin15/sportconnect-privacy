SportConnect privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

SportConnect is an iPhone app that helps people aged 18 and over find others to play sports with nearby. It is a student project made for Fontys University of Applied Sciences. This page explains which personal data the app uses, why, and what your rights are.

Who is responsible

Hugo Molin, student at Fontys University of Applied Sciences, Eindhoven, the Netherlands. Questions about your data: hugomolin.mail@icloud.com.

Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to show activities near you on the map and to fill in your city when you sign up. Your location is used on your phone only. It is not sent to us or stored, except the city you choose at sign-up. You can turn location access off at any time in the iPhone Settings app; the app keeps working, you then move the map yourself.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics tools.
  • We never sell or share your data with other companies.

Where your data is stored

The data is stored with Supabase, a database service, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union). Access is protected with security rules in the database, so every user can only change their own data and can only read what this page says they can see.

Legal basis

We use your data because it is needed to provide the app to you (performance of a contract, GDPR article 6(1)(b)). Location is only used with your permission (consent, article 6(1)(a)), which you can take back at any time.

How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your data as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile, then Delete account. This deletes your login, your name, date of birth and city, and removes you from every activity you joined. Activities you organise are handed over to the next person who joined; if nobody else joined, the activity is deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to see the data we have about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it. Email hugomolin.mail@icloud.com and you will get an answer within one month. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
